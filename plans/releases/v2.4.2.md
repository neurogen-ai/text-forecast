## Technical approach and risk

Architecture choices: no config surface and no contract change anywhere.
Synchronous logging makes each call an HTTP request in the calling thread.
The id guard is per batch (`torch.isnan(batch.id).all()`), shared by both
feeds through one `_feed_ids` helper, so `return_id=False` configs skip the
feed instead of tripping the tracker's NaN rejection. The latch is an
instance attribute, deliberately not reset by `clear()`: it is diagnostics
state, not gathered data.

Friction points and descope triggers:

- MAJOR. The deadlock diagnosis is inferred from logs, not observed under a
  debugger. Synchronous logging removes the diagnosed mechanism (MLflow
  async threads alive at the dataloader's per-epoch fork; forked workers
  inherit locks held mid-request and deadlock). If the hang persists, the
  diagnosis was incomplete. Trigger: the owner's smoke test (`--subsample
  24`, watch the train bar cross 16/24 into epoch 2 without hanging). If it
  hangs again, the next fix is `persistent_workers=True` on both loaders,
  then `num_workers=0` for smoke runs; neither is in this release.
- MAJOR. Synchronous per-batch logging costs one blocking HTTP round trip
  per training batch. Immaterial against the local tracking server;
  material for remote tracking on the modal runtime. Trigger: if per-batch
  logging shows up in step time at the first 2.5.0 modal dry run, batch the
  metrics per epoch via `log_batch`; never re-enable async logging without
  a fork-safe worker scheme.
- MINOR. `graph_embed_class` trains with `return_id=False`, so its
  `train_ids` store stays empty and now logs one WARNING per run instead of
  one ERROR per epoch. Design-expected condition, no trigger.
- MINOR. The eval pipeline's window loaders fork per window under the same
  mechanism; covered by the same change, no separate work.

Cost: small. Three one-line logging changes, one guard method, one latch,
restored tests. Smaller than any change 2.3 absorbed.

## Scope-change events

- STRUCK 2026-09-09 (owner decision): the synchronous-logging checklist
  item. The owner applied the synchronous flips (`synchronous=False`
  removed at the tracker's per-batch `train_loss-batch`, the engine's
  per-epoch metrics, and the eval pipeline's per-window metrics), ran the
  acceptance smoke, and the training hang reproduced with synchronous
  logging in place. This fires the MAJOR friction trigger recorded under
  "Technical approach and risk": the fork-deadlock diagnosis was
  incomplete. The flips were reverted; `synchronous=False` (async
  logging) remains the status quo and is not the hang's cause. The hung
  diagnosis and next hypotheses (`persistent_workers=True`, then
  `num_workers=0`, both still parked below) move to a new hang-
  investigation proposal; any such proposal must demand observed evidence
  (py-spy/faulthandler dump or debugger observation at hang time), not
  log inference. Numbering unchanged: 2.4.2 ships the surviving id-feed
  and latch items; 2.4.3 and 2.4.4 are unaffected.
